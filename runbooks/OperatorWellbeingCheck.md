# Operator Wellbeing & Operational Alignment Runbook
* **Status:** Enforced Routine
* **Objective:** Prevent burnout, eliminate low-value friction, and align technical output with core personal priorities.

## 1. The Adrenaline Trap Mitigation
* **Paced Execution:** Fast automation cycles must not override deliberate, reflective decision-making. 
* **Family & Personal Anchors:** Professional success is meaningless without personal alignment. Core family priorities and mental grounding take permanent precedence over system maintenance loops.

## 2. Routine Audit Checklist
1. **Friction Review:** Identify and sever low-value tasks or toxic commitments that drain operator energy.
2. **Downtime Enforcement:** Schedule intentional offline periods to reset focus and maintain long-term leadership clarity.
