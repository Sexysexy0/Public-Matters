# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We, as members, contributors, and leaders of the Sexysexy0 organization, pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, sexual orientation, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, or religion.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, trolling, or abusive comments
- Public or private sexual harassment
- Publishing others’ private information without consent
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement Responsibilities
- Supreme Steward (Vinvin) and designated maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards.
- They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed unacceptable.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all repositories, issues, pull requests, and community spaces under Sexysexy0.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to:
- Email: conduct@sexysexy0.org
- GitHub Security Advisories (private)

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
