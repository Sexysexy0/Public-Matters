# 📜 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Public Matters project a harassment‑free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, sexual orientation, level of experience, education, socio‑economic status, nationality, or religion.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, trolling, or abusive comments
- Publishing others’ private information without consent
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team at **conduct@publicmatters.org**.  
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
