## Controls adopted

- Governance and decision rights: Apply repository-scoped decision rights,
  documented review, and reserved-decision principles where applicable.
- Records and evidence: Distinguish source artifacts, local deployments,
  public testnet deployments, production deployments, failed attempts,
  deprecated artifacts, and evidence requiring reconciliation.
- Security and access: Apply least privilege, protect local credentials,
  review collaborator and automation access, and keep private keys, seed
  phrases, recovery information, and secret material outside public files.
- Change and incident management: Use documented, reviewable changes and
  proportionate records for material security or operational events.
- Privacy and public-disclosure boundary: Do not place credentials, private
  signer material, personal data, private contracts, sensitive evidence, live
  access inventories, or recovery information in public repository materials.
- Dependency and third-party integration review: Document material tooling,
  package, workflow, platform, and service dependencies before relying on them.
- Delegation and reserved decisions: Document material internal delegations and
  subject reserved decisions to additional review or explicit authorization.

documentation are not by themselves proof of real-world authority, service
operation, legal effect, or external adoption. Such claims require separate,
