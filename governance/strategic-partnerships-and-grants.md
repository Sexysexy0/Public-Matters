# Strategic Partnerships and Resource Mobilization Governance

## Scope
This framework governs how external partnerships, funding opportunities, grants, and resource mobilization pipelines are identified, vetted, tracked, and managed to support our organizational mission.

## Strategic Objectives
* **Ecosystem Mapping:** Systematically identify and evaluate potential partners across government bodies, international development organizations, NGOs, and the private sector.
* **Grant Lifecycle Management:** Maintain rigorous oversight over proposal development, budget tracking, compliance with donor regulations (e.g., international funding standards), and reporting.

## Governance Rules
* All major funding proposals and partnership agreements must undergo compliance reviews aligned with our core governance standards.
* Maintain an active pipeline tracker for open grants, fellowships, and matching funds to ensure proactive submission and timely execution.
