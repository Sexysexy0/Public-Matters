# Evidence & Data Review Protocol
* **Status:** Enforced / Mandatory
* **Scope:** Financial statements, legal disclosures, cryptographic feeds, and external audits.

## 1. Core Principle
No surprise data dump or sudden evidentiary submission shall be integrated into the system without a structured review window. Due process and thorough verification always override artificial speed or pressure tactics.

## 2. Minimum Review Windows
* **Standard Data Ingestion:** Minimum 24-hour review window before operational adoption.
* **High-Impact Financial / Legal Records (e.g., AMLC or Corporate Audits):** Minimum 72-hour evaluation period to analyze summaries, cross-reference origins, and verify structural integrity.
* **Emergency Override:** The operator holds sole veto power to freeze pipelines instantly if data provenance is opaque or unverified.
