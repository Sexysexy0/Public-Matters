# Knowledge Toolmaker Model

## Purpose

Public-Matters is not only a collection of code and contracts. It is also a knowledge-toolmaking archive: a place where complex ideas are translated into usable frameworks, prototypes, explanations, and public-interest systems.

## Core principle

I do not only search for answers. I build tools, frameworks, and interpretive maps that help people find, understand, and use answers.

## The toolmaker role

The toolmaker identifies a problem, studies the context, connects ideas across fields, and produces something usable by other people. The output may be:

- a Solidity contract;
- a repository;
- a governance framework;
- a policy proposal;
- a decision loop;
- a public explanation;
- a dashboard or interface;
- a teaching or reflection prompt.

## Two layers of distribution

### Public utility layer

The material should be understandable and useful to a broad audience. It should explain the problem, possible solutions, limitations, and safe use.

### Private or system-specific layer

The author may attach personal meaning, internal references, symbolic naming, or private notes. This layer should remain private when disclosure would compromise safety, privacy, access control, or personal boundaries.

## Interpretation rule

A hidden layer that I intentionally authored in my own work is a design feature. A hidden layer I infer in someone else's work is an interpretation and must not be treated as confirmed intent without direct evidence.

## Workflow

1. Notice a problem or useful idea.
2. Gather reliable context.
3. Separate facts from assumptions.
4. Identify the people or communities affected.
5. Translate the idea into a usable form.
6. Test the design with realistic risks and alternative explanations.
7. Preserve authorship and provenance.
8. Publish or share only what is safe and ready.
9. Collect feedback.
10. Improve the tool.

## Ethics and safety

Knowledge tools should expand understanding and agency, not manipulate people. Do not expose private keys, credentials, confidential access, or unnecessary identity data. Do not describe a prototype as a live deployment. Do not present personal interpretation as institutional endorsement.

## Relationship to Public-Matters

Public-Matters is the foundational archive for this model. Its contracts, manifests, dashboards, governance artifacts, and tagged releases show how ideas can move from symbolic or conceptual form into structured technical experiments.

## Short description

Public-Matters is an ideas-led knowledge-toolmaking laboratory: it translates complex observations into frameworks, code, governance concepts, and public-interest prototypes while preserving privacy and provenance.
