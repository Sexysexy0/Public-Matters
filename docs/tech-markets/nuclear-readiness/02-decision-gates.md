# Decision Gates

## Gates

- G0 — Validate the energy problem and demand forecast.
- G1 — Complete site, seismic, tsunami, volcanic, and environmental review.
- G2 — Compare BNPP, new reactors, SMRs, renewables, storage, and grid alternatives.
- G3 — Complete independent safety, security, and safeguards review.
- G4 — Approve lifecycle financing and consumer protections.
- G5 — Pass procurement integrity and conflict-of-interest review.
- G6 — Authorize construction or rehabilitation only after all prior gates pass.
- G7 — Authorize commissioning and fuel loading.
- G8 — Authorize grid connection and commercial operation.
- G9 — Continue operation only after recurring inspection and audit.

## Required record

Every gate decision must identify the gate, decision, evidence IDs, owner, independent reviewer, conflicts, conditions, review deadline, correction path, and public record hash.

## Hard stop

A failed or incomplete mandatory gate pauses the program. Schedule or political urgency cannot override an unresolved safety, legal, cost, or conflict finding.
