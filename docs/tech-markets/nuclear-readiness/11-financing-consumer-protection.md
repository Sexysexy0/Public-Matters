# Financing and Consumer Protection

Publish CAPEX, OPEX, financing assumptions, delay sensitivity, exchange-rate exposure, insurance, waste, decommissioning, grid, and de-risking costs.

## Consumer safeguards

- No blank-check guarantee.
- Independent value-for-money review.
- Public tariff-impact model.
- Disclosure of contingent liabilities.
- Stop-loss and reapproval triggers for material cost or schedule variance.
