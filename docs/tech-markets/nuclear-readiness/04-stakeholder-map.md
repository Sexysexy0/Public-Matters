# Stakeholder Map

Stakeholders include affected communities, national and local government, the independent regulator, energy and grid institutions, workers, emergency services, health institutions, universities, vendors, civil society, consumer groups, and international technical partners.

## Engagement requirements

- Publish plain-language evidence and unresolved questions.
- Record comments, responses, and changes made.
- Protect complainants and whistleblowers from retaliation.
- Do not treat consultation as a substitute for technical licensing.
