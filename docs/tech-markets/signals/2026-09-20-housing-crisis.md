# Metro Manila Housing Crisis

**Date observed:** 2026-09-20
**Source:** ULI 2025, PIDS study, PSA data, Reyes & Trinidad (2023)
**Type:** policy / structural analysis

## What we saw

Metro Manila among world's most unaffordable housing markets:
- Average salary: PHP 22,000/month
- Attainable home: PHP 1.3M max (5x annual income)
- Condo: 19.8x annual income
- Townhouse: 33.4x annual income
- Singapore public housing: 4.3x (80% of population)
- Studio rent: PHP 25,000-35,000 (>100% of salary)
- 4.5M homeless Filipinos
- 6.5M housing backlog (2022), 10M projected (2028)

## Pattern

Financialization — housing treated as speculation asset,
not human right. State-facilitated market failure.

## Governance principle

Housing is a human right. Policy must:
1. De-financialize (vacancy tax, anti-speculation)
2. Build public housing directly
3. Decongest Metro Manila
