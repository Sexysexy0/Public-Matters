# Silence Is Not Neutral

**Date observed:** 2026-09-18
**Source:** Substack article + Bonhoeffer quote
**Type:** observation

## What we saw

"I don't take sides" = silent complicity. Bonhoeffer: not to speak is to
speak; not to act is to act.

## Pattern

Passive stance in the face of active harm functions as endorsement.

## Governance principle

Non-action must be recorded with equal weight to action. Abstention is
not neutral.
