# Status Boundary

Public-Matters contains source code, tests, research, policy-prototyping
materials, local-development records, and public testnet deployment records.

Ethereum Sepolia and Polygon Amoy are public test networks. A testnet deployment
is not a production or mainnet deployment.

Repository materials do not by themselves establish legal authority, government
authority, institutional adoption, regulatory approval, custody, financial
authority, external service operation, or authority over third parties.

See `docs/ARTIFACT-STATUS.md` for the status taxonomy and evidence boundary.
