# Community Housing & Micro-Amortization Program (Tahanan Para sa Pamilya)

## Overview
A zero-barrier social housing acquisition framework tailored specifically for minimum wage earners currently trapped in renting, designed to replace rigid banking requirements with community-backed micro-amortizations.

## Target Beneficiaries & Requirements
1. **Employment Status**: Minimum wage earners whose current income restricts traditional bank housing loans.
2. **Housing Status**: Currently renting a residential space with high monthly cash outflow.
3. **Family Structure**: Married or single parents with dependent children.
4. **No Membership Fee**: Completely free to register; no mandatory savings lock-in before qualifying.

## Barangay & District Announcement Protocol
- **Direct Outreach**: Utilizing local barangay halls, community bulletin boards, and local public address systems to announce enrollment schedules.
- **On-Ground Validation**: Streamlined verification by local community leaders to certify renters without requiring bureaucratic tax documents or payslips.
- **Affordable Plan Structure**: Tailored monthly payments starting at a comfortable baseline (e.g., matching or lower than current rent) that goes directly toward property equity.
