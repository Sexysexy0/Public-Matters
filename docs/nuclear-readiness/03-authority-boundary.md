# Authority Boundary

| Function | Responsible authority | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| National energy policy | Executive and legislature | Cannot override independent safety licensing |
| Energy planning | Energy department | Cannot issue nuclear safety approval |
| Nuclear safety and licensing | Independent nuclear regulator | Cannot select vendors or own project finances |
| Consumer rates | Energy-market regulator | Cannot waive nuclear safety requirements |
| Grid integration | Grid operator | Cannot select vendor or certify safety |
| Procurement | Contracting authority | Cannot alter technical safety criteria |
| Independent review | Accredited reviewer | Cannot award or amend the reviewed contract |
| Public evidence | Evidence office | Cannot suppress adverse findings |

## Separation rule

No single office may define the need, select the vendor, approve safety, control the money, and certify completion.
