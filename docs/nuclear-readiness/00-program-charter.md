# Nuclear Readiness Program Charter

## Purpose

Evaluate whether nuclear deployment is safe, lawful, affordable, resilient, and publicly accountable.

## Scope

BNPP rehabilitation, new reactors, SMRs, renewable and storage alternatives, grid integration, financing, safety, security, waste, emergency response, workforce, procurement, and public evidence.

## Non-goals

This program does not authorize construction, rehabilitation, fuel purchase, public guarantees, or operation.

## Decision rule

No irreversible commitment proceeds until the applicable decision gate passes, evidence is independently reviewed, conflicts are disclosed, and the public record is published.

## Core principles

- Safety and regulatory independence come before schedule.
- Evidence must be versioned, reviewable, and correctable.
- Lifecycle costs include financing, waste, decommissioning, and emergency readiness.
- Nuclear is evaluated alongside credible non-nuclear alternatives.
- Authority must be separated across policy, regulation, procurement, finance, and audit.
