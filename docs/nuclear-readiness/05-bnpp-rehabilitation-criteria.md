# BNPP Rehabilitation Criteria

BNPP rehabilitation remains an option under assessment, not an approved outcome.

## Minimum evidence

- Independent structural, mechanical, electrical, and instrumentation assessment.
- Current seismic, tsunami, volcanic, flooding, and climate-risk assessment.
- Compliance gap against current safety standards.
- Cost, schedule, spare-parts, workforce, security, and decommissioning plan.
- Comparison with a new site and non-nuclear alternatives.
- Independent regulator determination before any irreversible commitment.

Any material unresolved safety or financial uncertainty is a hold condition.
