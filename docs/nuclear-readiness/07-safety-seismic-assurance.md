# Safety and Seismic Assurance

Safety claims require site-specific evidence, independent review, regulator acceptance, and recurring inspection. Generic claims about modern reactor safety are not substitutes for a Philippine site safety case.

## Required controls

- Defense-in-depth safety case.
- Seismic, tsunami, volcanic, flooding, and climate analysis.
- Human factors and severe-accident analysis.
- Physical and cybersecurity integration.
- Independent peer review.
- Public summary with sensitive details appropriately protected.
