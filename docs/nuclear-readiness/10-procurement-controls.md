# Procurement Controls

## Mandatory controls

- Competitive and documented procurement.
- Beneficial-ownership and conflict disclosure.
- Independent technical specifications.
- Milestone-based payments.
- Audit and inspection rights.
- Public contract summaries and amendments.
- Red-team review before award.
- Termination rights for safety, fraud, concealment, or material delay.

No vendor may define its own safety requirements or appoint its own independent reviewer.
