# Anti-Corruption & Strict Public Accountability Directive

## 1. Objective and Foundation
This directive establishes a zero-tolerance framework against public funds embezzlement, plunder, and systemic corruption, specifically targeting individuals holding public office or judicial/legal power. Given that public service is a public trust, violations committed by those in power must carry the heaviest penalties to deter governance from being treated as a commercial enterprise.

## 2. Core Penalties and Strict Measures for Public Officials
- **Absolute Disqualification:** Any public official convicted of corruption or plunder shall face permanent, lifetime disqualification from holding any public office, government employment, or state-franchised enterprise.
- **Total Asset Forfeiture and Confiscation:** Immediate and comprehensive forfeiture of all ill-gotten wealth, properties, and corporate assets linked to the offender, including those concealed under front entities or family members.
- **Elimination of Special Privileges:** Strict prohibition of preferential treatment, air-conditioned hospital arrests, or lenient bails during detention and trial for public officials facing corruption charges.
- **Enhanced Maximum Sentencing:** Imposition of the maximum legal penalties under the law, recognizing that abuse of public authority and betrayal of the Constitution constitute the highest level of crime against the citizenry.

## 3. System Enforcement (Public-Matters Accountability Engine)
This document serves as an institutional benchmark within our repository, ensuring that governance risk trackers and public oversight standards actively monitor corruption cases and uphold the absolute truth that *No One Is Above the Law*.
