# Subsistence Offense Immunity & Human Dignity Policy

## Overview
This policy establishes that no individual shall be arrested, detained, or criminalized for petty subsistence actions—such as taking a piece of fruit or basic food items—out of immediate hunger or to feed a recovering, starving family. 

## Core Principles
1. **Right to Subsistence over Property Technicalities**: Human life and the basic necessity to eat supersede rigid property laws in times of systemic poverty and destitution.
2. **Prohibition of Petty Criminalization**: Law enforcement and local authorities are barred from arresting small subsistence offenders. Instead, the occurrence must trigger an immediate state welfare and food subsidy response.
3. **State and Institutional Failure Acknowledgment**: When a father is forced to steal a banana for his postpartum wife, the failure lies in a broken socio-economic system, not in the desperation of a starving citizen.
