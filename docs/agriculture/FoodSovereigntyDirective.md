# Food Sovereignty & Agricultural Protection Directive

## 1. Objective and Foundation
This directive establishes a policy framework to combat systemic food insecurity, agricultural neglect, and massive food wastage in the Philippines. By shifting the paradigm from mere food security—which often relies heavily on foreign importation—to **food sovereignty**, this framework prioritizes local agricultural production, farmers' welfare, and community-controlled food systems.

## 2. Core Principles and Policy Mandates
- **Prioritization of Local Producers:** Mandating preferential support, subsidies, and market access for domestic farmers and fisherfolk to eradicate agricultural poverty and stunting among farming families.
- **Reduction of Food Wastage:** Establishing efficient supply chain networks, cold storage facilities, and redistribution frameworks to rescue the millions of tons of edible food wasted annually and redirect them to food-insecure regions.
- **Anti-Import Dependency:** Restricting unregulated and excessive food importations that depress local farmgate prices and undermine national agricultural self-reliance.

## 3. System Enforcement (Public-Matters Accountability Engine)
This document serves as an official institutional register within our repository to monitor agricultural policies, advocate for food sovereignty laws, and hold state agencies accountable for nutritional security and rural development.
