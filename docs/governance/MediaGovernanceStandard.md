# Media Governance & Ethical Reporting Standard

## 1. Objective and Foundation
This policy aims to promote honest, unbiased, and accountable journalism. Under this framework, news reporting must be anchored strictly on factual truth, free from any filter, manipulation, or undue influence of vested interests, enabling the public to form their own conclusions based on comprehensive information.

## 2. Core Principles of Reporting
- **Balanced & Dual-Perspective Reporting:** One-sided reporting or favoring a single camp is strictly prohibited. Every issue must include statements, reactions, or context from all involved parties.
- **Freedom from Bias and Manipulation:** News must be reported transparently without suppression, distortion, or malicious framing designed to protect powerful individuals or institutions.
- **Real-Time and Timely Access:** Ensuring prompt and accurate delivery of hot, updated, and latest news without unjustified delays or politically motivated censorship.

## 3. Institutional and Media Accountability
1. **Transparency in Public Events:** Media entities and official broadcasters have a binding duty to clearly explain ongoing processes, laws, and rulings from courts or government bodies to the citizenry.
2. **Accountability for Silence or Omission:** Deliberately withholding news, ignoring critical public issues, or maintaining biased silence on matters of national interest constitutes a direct breach of public trust.
