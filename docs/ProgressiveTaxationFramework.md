# Progressive Taxation & State Subsidy Framework

## Overview
Establishing the financial mechanisms required to fund MSME transition buffers and public social services through equitable wealth distribution.

## Funding & Implementation Mechanisms
1. **Wealth Tax & Corporate Contributions**: Imposing higher levies on ultra-high-net-worth individuals and massive corporations to fund worker wage subsidies.
2. **VAT Exemptions on Essentials**: Removing value-added taxes on basic food items, electricity, water, and fuel to immediately boost worker purchasing power.
3. **Free Public Services**: Subsidizing education, healthcare, and public transport to relieve family living costs.
