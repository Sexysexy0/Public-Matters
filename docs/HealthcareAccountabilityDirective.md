# Healthcare & PhilHealth Konsulta Accountability Directive

## 1. Objective and Foundation
This directive establishes a transparent monitoring framework for public healthcare programs, specifically addressing discrepancies, unauthorized facility assignments, and capitation payment allocation under the PhilHealth Konsulta program. Public health funds must directly serve the citizenry without administrative exploitation or geographical mismatch.

## 2. Core Principles and Citizen Protections
- **Right to Facility Choice:** Citizens retain the absolute right to select their primary-care provider nearest to their actual residence, prohibiting unauthorized cross-town or ghost facility assignments.
- **Capitation and Subsidy Transparency:** Rigorous auditing of capitation payments and financial incentives to ensure funds are tied to legitimate, consented, and delivered healthcare services rather than administrative manipulation.
- **Public Awareness and Verification:** Empowering communities to audit their registration records, ensuring transparency against deceptive token incentives and misplaced medical assignments.

## 3. System Enforcement (Public-Matters Accountability Engine)
This document serves as an official institutional register within our repository to track local healthcare governance issues, protect patient rights, and demand absolute accountability from public health administrators.
