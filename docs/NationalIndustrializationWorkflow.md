# National Industrialization & Economic Overhaul Workflow

## Overview
This document outlines the shift from a cheap-labor dependent economy to a high-value creation industrial framework supporting the ₱1,300 daily living wage standard.

## Strategic Pillars
1. **Abolition of Regional Wage Boards**: Transitioning to a uniform national living wage standard indexed to cost-of-living adjustments.
2. **Local Production & Manufacturing**: Building domestic industries across critical supply chains to eliminate import reliance.
3. **Agricultural Modernization**: Lowering food prices through state-backed infrastructure and direct support for local farmers, reducing daily household expenditure burdens.
