# Policy Directive: Opposition to Arbitrary Platform Censorship & Institutional Overreach

## 1. Context & Background
Recent arbitrary directives by regulatory bodies (such as DICT and NTC) to censor communication and productivity platforms (e.g., Discord and other community tools) have caused massive disruptions to legitimate work, education, and daily livelihoods. Furthermore, such overreaches trigger severe systemic backlashes, including infrastructure vulnerabilities and data exposures.

## 2. Core Principles
- **Digital Freedom & Open Access:** Rejection of blanket bans and unverified censorship sprees that stifle public communication and economic activity.
- **Institutional Accountability:** Holding regulatory agencies and telecom executives accountable for privacy failures, poor security postures, and abuse of regulatory power.
- **Resilient Infrastructure:** Ensuring that public networks are protected against retaliatory cyber threats rather than being weaponized for suppression.

## 3. Action Plan & Demands
1. **Immediate Cessation:** Lift unjust blocks on community platforms used by citizens, developers, and students.
2. **Security Audits:** Mandate transparent investigations into telecommunication infrastructure vulnerabilities following reported data leaks[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span).
3. **Legal & Administrative Recourse:** File formal accountability grievances against negligent public officials and entities enabling institutional failure.
