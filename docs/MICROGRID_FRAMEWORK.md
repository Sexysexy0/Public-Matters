# Barangay Micro-Grid & Utility Cost Relief Framework

## Overview
A high-autonomy, transparent utility governance model designed to relieve surging living costs for families in Malolos and surrounding communities through decentralized local energy deployment and verifiable milestone auditing.

## Core Pillars
1. **Localized Energy Co-ops:** Decentralized micro-grids to bypass middleman transmission markups.
2. **On-Chain Accountability:** Smart contract tracking (`MicroGridTracker.sol`) ensuring zero "ghost projects" and transparent fund allocation.
3. **Equitable Cost Relief:** Direct community distribution metrics to stabilize household energy expenses.
