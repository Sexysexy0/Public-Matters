# Singapore's "Reset" — Success Trap Signal

**Date observed:** 2026-09-20
**Source:** Video analysis on Singapore economic model
**Type:** policy / economic history

## What we saw

Singapore (1965): poverty, no resources, insecurity.
Transformation: foreign investment + anti-corruption + education + public housing + CPF savings.
Success: world-class economy, widespread wealth.

Current crisis (2020s-2026):
- Hyper-competitive education (billions on tuition)
- Longest work hours in developed world
- Resale housing market unaffordable for young families
- Mental health crisis ("rat race" feeling)
- PM Lawrence Wong: fundamental "reset" needed

## Pattern

Success trap: the model that created prosperity now creates new problems.
- 1965 crisis -> state-led transformation -> success -> new crisis -> reset
- Institutional adaptation is required, not just growth

## Governance principle

Every successful model has trade-offs. Institutional adaptation must be continuous.
Success is not a destination — it's a moving target.
