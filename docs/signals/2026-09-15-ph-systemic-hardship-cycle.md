# PH Systemic Hardship Cycle

**Date observed:** 2026-09-15
**Source:** Social media essay "LAHAT PAHIRAP SA PINAS"
**Type:** post

## What we saw

Education cycle: 18 years schooling -> 10+ pre-employment requirements ->
experience trap -> entry-level tax burden -> consumption debt cycle.
Every stage adds friction instead of reducing it.

## Pattern

Systemic friction amplification. Each institutional step (education,
permits, hiring, taxation) adds cost without removing the next barrier.

## Governance principle

Friction reduction: each step should reduce, not compound, barriers to
productive participation.
