# Governance Framework

Sexysexy0 functions as an enterprise sandbox under the stewardship of Vinvin (Emervin V. Gueco).  
All repositories are governed by the principles of fairness, transparency, and accountability.

## Structure
- Supreme Steward: Vinvin
- Core Developers: Responsible for contract design and audits
- QA Team: Responsible for test coverage and resilience checks
- DevOps Team: Responsible for CI/CD and deployment pipelines

## Principles
- Public Matters Covenant: All repos align with systemic governance logic.
- PPP Treaties: Partnerships, Policies, and Protocols encoded in smart contracts.
- Compliance: All repos reference ISO, SOC, and CSA standards.
