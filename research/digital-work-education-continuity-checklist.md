# Digital Work and Education Continuity Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist helps organizations, educators, workers, students, communities, and service providers reduce disruption when access to a digital communication or collaboration service is restricted, unavailable, or degraded.

It is a continuity-planning tool. It does not advise bypassing lawful restrictions or accessing systems without authorization.

## Before disruption

- Identify services critical to work, education, project coordination, support communities, and accessibility.
- Maintain approved alternative communication channels.
- Keep essential documents, schedules, contact lists, and learning materials in accessible, authorized locations.
- Document service dependencies, including authentication, file-sharing, meeting, community, and moderation functions.
- Establish institutional points of contact for service disruption.
- Communicate privacy-aware backup procedures to affected users.
- Test continuity plans without exposing credentials or confidential information.

## During disruption

- Confirm the scope, duration, and source of the disruption through reliable channels.
- Use approved backup communication methods.
- Inform affected workers, learners, partners, and community members promptly.
- Preserve essential records and document material impacts.
- Provide accessible alternatives for users with disability, language, bandwidth, or device constraints.
- Avoid spreading unverified explanations or instructions to circumvent restrictions.
- Escalate urgent safety, academic, employment, or service-delivery concerns through authorized channels.

## After restoration or resolution

- Confirm that normal service is available and that essential records remain intact.
- Collect documented reports of work, education, accessibility, and community impacts.
- Identify which dependencies lacked adequate alternatives.
- Update continuity contacts, documentation, and approved backup tools.
- Record lessons learned without publishing personal or sensitive information.
- Review whether the response was proportionate, transparent, and time-limited.
