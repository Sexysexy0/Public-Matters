# Pyrowave Source Register

**Created:** 2026-09-27
**Purpose:** Preserve an evidence trail for the Pyrowave latency–bandwidth review.

## Source classification

| Source class | Use | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Official Steam / Valve update | Feature existence, supported platforms, stated intent | May not provide independent benchmarks |
| Steam Remote Play community announcement | Beta availability and operational notes | Community context; may change quickly |
| Independent technical reporting | Architecture descriptions and testing context | Verify methods and distinguish reporting from measured evidence |
| Community posts and videos | Leads, reproduction reports, compatibility signals | Not authoritative; do not treat as conclusive |

## Initial sources to verify

| Source | Claim category | Confidence note |
|---|---|---|
| Steam Client Beta / Steam news | Experimental Pyrowave feature announcement | Primary source for release status |
| Steam Remote Play discussion group | Availability and platform notes | Primary community channel |
| Digital Foundry coverage | Technical analysis and benchmark context | Independent reporting; review methodology |
| Post-release Steam Beta notes | Bug fixes, GPU behavior, Linux/SteamOS changes | Primary source for subsequent changes |

## Research discipline

- Record publication or update date for every source.
- Record the date a source was accessed.
- Do not convert a beta claim into a stable-product guarantee.
- Do not treat one hardware setup as representative of all users.
- Separate observed facts, reported measurements, and hypotheses.
