# Game Wayfinding Playtest Template

## Purpose

Use this template to evaluate whether players can understand goals, identify plausible routes, navigate fairly, and receive appropriate levels of guidance.

This template does not require a minimal HUD or prohibit explicit navigation support. It helps teams determine whether guidance is understandable, accessible, optional where feasible, and proportionate to observed player needs.

## Participant reflection

Ask open questions without suggesting a preferred answer.

- What did you think your goal was?
- What made you choose the route you took?
- When did you feel confident about where to go?
- When did you feel uncertain or stuck?
- Did you notice any environmental cues that helped?
- Did you want more, less, or different guidance?
- Did the guidance feel helpful, distracting, or revealing?
- Did the level make you feel like you were exploring, following instructions, or both?
- What would you change?
