# Operations Ghost-Operator Audit Template

## Purpose

Use this template to identify, understand, and address undocumented operational dependencies without treating good-faith reporting as misconduct.

A record may describe a person-dependent action, workaround, credential, manual correction, script, spreadsheet, device configuration, exception process, access path, or other practice that is necessary for work to continue but is not adequately visible, owned, reviewed, or controlled.

Do not include unnecessary personal information, passwords, security secrets, private customer information, or operational details that could create new risk if disclosed.
