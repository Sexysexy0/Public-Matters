# Policy 02: Office for Citizenship Standards

## Overview
Defines the institutional standards for verifying existing citizenships, mitigating identity fraud, and enforcing compliance thresholds.

## Core Directives
1. **Compliance Mapping**: Integrates with corporate UBO and identity layers to cross-reference beneficial ownership and residency.
2. **Fraud Prevention**: Zero-tolerance protocols for dual-identity masking or undocumented corporate entities.
