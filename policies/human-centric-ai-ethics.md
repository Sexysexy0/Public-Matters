# Human-Centric AI and Digital Ethics Policy

## Overview
This policy establishes mandatory guidelines for the design, deployment, and utilization of artificial intelligence and automated digital systems within our operational framework. It ensures that technology serves to enhance human dignity rather than cause dehumanization, isolation, or exploitation.

## Core Principles
* **Dignity First:** Technology and automation must prioritize human well-being, creative expression, and social connection over mere productivity metrics.
* **Transparency and Accountability:** AI-driven tools and automated workflows must remain transparent in their decision-making processes, maintaining clear human oversight.
* **Mitigation of Dehumanization:** Systems must be continuously monitored to prevent the reduction of human experiences to cold, automated categorization.

## Operational Implementation
* Regular audits of automated systems to check for biases, privacy risks, and psychological isolation effects.
* Mandatory human-in-the-loop validation for critical decisions affecting community stakeholders and organizational partners.
