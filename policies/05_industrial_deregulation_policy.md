# Policy 05: Industrial Deregulation Policy

## Overview
Framework designed to remove administrative bottlenecks and unnecessary regulatory friction for local factories, small-to-medium enterprises, and industrial operators.

## Core Directives
1. **Friction Reduction**: Streamline business compliance using transparent digital tools.
2. **Autonomous Operations**: Empower local manufacturing and assembly units to operate under standardized safety and compliance thresholds without bureaucratic delays.
