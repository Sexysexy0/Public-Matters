# Policy 01: Border Control and Immigration Reform Framework

## Overview
This policy establishes the sovereign framework for rigorous border security, individual classification, and immigration verification protocols within the Public-Matters ecosystem.

## Core Directives
1. **Strict Identity Verification**: All inbound entities and individuals must undergo cryptographic or documented verification before receiving temporary or permanent status.
2. **Resource Allocation**: Border security metrics are directly tied to the sovereign registry (`SovereigntyPolicyRegistry.sol`).
3. **Auditability**: Every exception or waiver granted must be logged on-chain with multi-role approval (`AccessControlGovernance.sol`).
