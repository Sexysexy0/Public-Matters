# Policy 03: Sovereignty and Autonomy Protocol

## Overview
Outlines the operational boundaries to preserve national and institutional independence, preventing undue external influence from supranational bodies.

## Core Directives
1. **Code is Law & Institutional Supremacy**: Internal governance parameters (`rootGovernor`, `onlyAdmin`) supersede external lobbying or non-binding directives.
2. **Decentralized Execution**: Execution of sensitive protocols requires decentralized consensus via our multi-role framework.
