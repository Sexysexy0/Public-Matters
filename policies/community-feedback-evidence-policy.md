# Community Feedback Evidence Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how community, player, user, reviewer, and support feedback should be recorded, interpreted, prioritized, and communicated.

## Core rule

Feedback is a valuable signal of user experience. It does not automatically establish technical cause, legal fault, developer intent, majority preference, or a mandatory response.

## Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|
| `reported` | A source describes an experience | Record and investigate within stated limits |
| `observed` | A condition was directly observed in a documented context | Describe the observed condition and context |
| `reproduced` | A condition was independently repeated with documented steps | Support a confirmed-issue decision |
| `disputed` | Credible sources conflict or context is incomplete | Preserve uncertainty and seek clarification |
| `unsupported` | Evidence is insufficient for reliance | Do not use for operational conclusions |
| `fictional_example` | Demonstration data only | Use only to test documentation or tooling |

## Source handling

- Record source type, date, context, and limitations.
- Distinguish direct reports from summaries, reposts, screenshots, and commentary.
- Do not count duplicate posts, copied text, coordinated campaigns, or bot activity as independent reports.
- Do not infer a population-wide preference from an unknown or non-representative sample.
- Preserve material dissent and negative feedback when responsibly stated.
- Use independent testing or telemetry only with appropriate privacy and authorization boundaries.

## Privacy and dignity

- Do not publish names, account identifiers, private messages, credentials, device identifiers, or unnecessary personal data.
- Redact sensitive information before sharing a record.
- Do not retaliate against reporters, critics, moderators, developers, competitors, or community members.
- Do not use the triage process to organize harassment, brigading, doxxing, or targeted abuse.

## Claim language

Use qualified language:

- A user reported the condition.
- The condition was observed in the documented environment.
- The issue was reproduced under the recorded steps.
- Available evidence is incomplete or disputed.
- This example is fictional and not a real report.

Avoid unqualified language:

- Everyone agrees.
- This proves the cause.
- One comment represents all users.
- A competitor caused the issue.
- A report proves legal wrongdoing.

## Decision and response record

For material feedback, record:

1. Feedback identifier and category
2. Evidence status and limitations
3. Affected group and severity assessment
4. Reproduction or investigation status
5. Priority and rationale
6. Chosen response, owner, and review date
7. Expected outcome and rollback or reconsideration conditions
8. Public-response wording where applicable

## Review

Review closed or declined items when new evidence, changed conditions, a regression, or a material affected-user report appears.
