# AI & Data Center Moratorium & Governance Policy

## Overview
In alignment with strict accountability guardrails and protection against systemic societal risks, this policy establishes an immediate pause, limit, and full stop on unverified advanced AI development, autonomous model scaling, and heavy data center infrastructure expansions that threaten resource resilience, employment stability, and public security.

## Core Mandates & Safeguards
1. **Halt on Unchecked Scaling:** Immediate suspension of advanced AI training runs and deployment pipelines lacking verifiable ethical audits.
2. **Data Center Expansion Limits:** Hard caps on energy-intensive data center projects that compromise local grid reliability or community resource allocation.
3. **Risk Mitigation Over Speed:** Prioritization of human agency, labor protection, and ecological preservation over reckless technological acceleration.
