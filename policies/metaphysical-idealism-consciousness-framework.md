# Metaphysical Idealism and Consciousness-Centric Systems Framework

## Core Premise
Moving beyond reductive physicalism, this framework recognizes that consciousness is primary, and what we perceive as material reality or institutional structures are expressions of mental activity within a broader interconnected substrate.

## Key Principles
* **Primacy of Consciousness:** Rejects purely materialistic or mechanistic views of human beings, treating individual and collective agency as fundamental, irreducible realities rather than mere biological byproducts.
* **Interconnectedness ("Mind at Large"):** Acknowledges the intrinsic linkage between individuals and systems, moving away from isolated competition toward holistic, cooperative synchronization.
* **The Filter Hypothesis in Systems Design:** Views organizational structures, technologies, and governance models as channels or "filters" designed to align local execution with higher-order principles of truth, justice, and meaning.

## Operational Application
1. **Holistic Problem Solving:** Design policies and institutional frameworks that account for qualitative human experience, psychological well-being, and ethical purpose rather than relying strictly on quantitative metrics.
2. **Resist Dehumanization:** Ensure that automation, digital tools, and institutional processes do not reduce human value to mechanical inputs, preserving the dignity of sovereign consciousness.
